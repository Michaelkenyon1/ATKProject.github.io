America's Test Kitchen Initiative

Saving a Dying Art: Reconnecting Gen Z to Cooking

A streamlined strategy to transform daily food friction into lifelong kitchen confidence through modern media.

1. The Problem: Cooking is Becoming a Dying Skill

For my generation, home cooking is rapidly becoming a lost art. Between delivery services, pre-packaged meal kits, and busy schedules, convenience has quietly replaced creativity. Many of us are entering adulthood without real kitchen confidence or the opportunity to fall in love with cooking from scratch.

When we do look for inspiration, we go straight to TikTok and YouTube. On these platforms, viral hacks get views, but they often fail in real life. America's Test Kitchen holds the key with bulletproof cooking science that actually works. By bringing the authority of America's Test Kitchen into modern formats, we can make cooking an exciting and accessible part of daily life.

2. Flagship YouTube Series: ATK MythBusters

An eight to twelve minute high-energy series pairing two test cooks to react to, recreate, and scientifically test viral recipes live in the kitchen.

0:00 to 1:30

The React and Setup

Test cooks watch viral social media recipes and share their initial impressions and skepticism.

1:30 to 5:00

The Side-by-Side Test

Recreating the viral recipe as instructed versus testing the refined and scientifically proven method from America's Test Kitchen.

5:00 to 8:00

Deep-Dive Science Breakdown

Explaining why it works using thermal graphics, texture tests, and clear visual science.

8:00 to 10:00

The Verdict and The ATK Fix

Delivering a final rating of Busted or Confirmed, followed by releasing the ultimate beginner-friendly recipe.
